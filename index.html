⚡ Super Simple Setup Guide (For Everyone!)

Set up MindfulShift on your computer or phone in under 60 seconds — no tech skills needed!

DESKTOP / LAPTOP
💻

Chrome / Edge / Brave

Get MindfulShift on your computer browser in 3 super simple steps:

  1. Download Code: Go to our GitHub page ➔ click green Code button ➔ click Download ZIP (and unzip it!).
  2. Open Extensions: Open Chrome and type chrome://extensions in the top bar. Turn ON "Developer mode" in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Load Unpacked: Click the "Load unpacked" button and select the extension folder!
🎉 BOOM! Now YouTube Shorts & IG Reels are blocked automatically!
ANDROID PHONE
📱

Android Phones & Tablets

Use MindfulShift directly on your phone like a real app:

  1. Open Page: Open this exact website in your Chrome browser on your Android phone.
  2. Tap 3 Dots: Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  3. Add to Home Screen: Tap "Add to Home screen" (or Install App).
IPHONE & IPAD
🍎

iPhone & iPad

Set up MindfulShift on your iPhone in 2 quick taps:

  1. Open Safari: Open this exact website in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Share Button: Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow 📤) at the bottom.
  3. Add to Home Screen: Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"!
