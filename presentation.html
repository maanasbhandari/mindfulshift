The Challenge

Why Social Media Addiction is Unbroken

Traditional blockers fail because they treat human psychology like a simple wall.

🎰
The Variable Reward Trap
Algorithms continuously deliver unpredictable dopamine hits. Opening Reels requires zero intentional effort, bypassing conscious decision making.
🛑
Punitive Blockers Backfire
Standard blockers completely lock apps, causing friction frustration. Users quickly disable or uninstall them when feeling bored or stressed.
Our Innovation

The MindfulShift 3-Step Engine

Replacing passive brain-rot with healthy micro-interventions.

🛑
1. Intentional Gate
Intercepts Reels/Shorts with a 3-second cognitive pause: "Why are you opening this right now?"
⚡
2. Dopamine Swap
Redirects boredom into a 30-second micro-challenge (coding trivia, math puzzle, or box breathing reset).
🎨
3. Auto-Grayscale & AI Roast
Scrolling past 10 mins slowly turns video grayscale & triggers a witty AI avatar roast to snap you back.
